 2. Opening — Enter Veyra
 Scene 1 — Entering the village
 Night. Elias enters Veyra carrying the Echo Lantern. Do not reveal his full face yet.
 SFX Footsteps. 
 SFX Wind. 
 SFX Lantern glass softly rattling. 
 Scene 2 — The clock tower
 VISUAL Clock reads 2:17 AM. The second hand is frozen. 
 SFX DONG. 
 SFX DONG. 
 Narration The clock tower had been silent for ten years. 
 Narration Tonight, it rang again. 
 Scene 3 — The toy
 VISUAL A small child's toy rolls down the empty street and stops. 
 SFX click... click... click... 
 Scene 4 — The window
 VISUAL A mysterious figure is briefly visible behind a window, then disappears. 
 SFX WHOOSH 
 DIRECTION No dialogue. Cut to black. 
 3. Ten years ago — comic history
 Panel 1 — Veyra before the incident
 Narration Ten years ago, Veyra was different. 
 Narration It was a quiet village. 
 Narration People lived here. 
 Narration They worked here. 
 Narration They built their lives here. 
 Panel 2 — The night
 Narration Then one night, something happened. 
 VISUAL Dark sky. Rain. Lightning. The village becomes chaotic. 
 Panel 3 — 2:17 AM
 VISUAL Clock tower: 2:17 AM. People vanish; streets become empty. 
 Narration At exactly 2:17 AM, the people of Veyra disappeared. 
 Panel 4 — The search
 Narration Days later, a search party entered the village. 
 Narration Everything had been left behind. 
 Narration There were no bodies. 
 Narration No clear signs of what had happened. 
 Narration No explanation. 
 Panel 5 — Case closed
 Narration The investigation continued for weeks. 
 Narration Nothing was discovered. 
 Narration Eventually, the case was closed. 
 4. Present-day village investigation
 Narration Ten years later, Veyra was still exactly where they had left it. 
 PLAYER Free village exploration begins. 
 Clock tower
 DISCOVERY Footprints lead toward the clock tower and stop suddenly. 
 CASEBOOK FOOTPRINTS — Multiple footprints leading toward the clock tower. 
 Blood-stained key
 DISCOVERY A small old key with dried blood is found near the footprints. 
 Narration Someone was here. 
 Narration And they were hurt. 
 CASEBOOK BLOOD-STAINED KEY — Found near the clock tower. 
 Bell mechanism
 DISCOVERY The bell rope appears untouched, although the bell rang. 
 Narration The bell rang. 
 Narration But nobody pulled it. 
 Clocks
 DISCOVERY Pocket watch, wall clock, school clock, church clock and broken watch all show 2:17. 
 CASEBOOK EVERY CLOCK STOPPED AT 2:17 
 Village records
 DISCOVERY Records contain 1:43, 1:58, 2:06, 2:11, 2:17 and 2:31. 
 Narration The clocks said 2:17. 
 Narration The records didn't. 
 Narration Something happened at 2:17. 
 Narration But 2:17 wasn't the whole story. 
 Other evidence
 DISCOVERY Burned photograph: Ivy, Luke, Hanna and a fourth lantern-carrying person whose face is scratched out. 
 DISCOVERY Torn village record. 
 DISCOVERY The same strange symbols appear near the clock tower, on doors and near the old well. 
 DISCOVERY Damaged recording: “...the lantern...” / “...don't activate it...” 
 Unknown woman — first encounter
 Elias enters an abandoned room. A woman is sitting there. He does not know who she is. She does not recognize him.
 UNKNOWN WOMAN Whatever you find here, don't trust the first memory you see. 
 VISUAL She disappears without fading. The chair remains and slowly rocks. 
 SFX Creeeak... 
 Narration I didn't know who she was. 
 Narration But I remembered what she said. 
 Important: The player does not learn that the unknown woman is Hanna at this point.

 VISUAL Schoolhouse. Ivy is not teaching; it is night and she is alone, gathering school materials and preparing to leave. 
 SFX Low hum. 
 UI MEMORY ECHO DETECTED

 VISUAL Everyone is moving toward the town square. 
 SFX DONG

 6. Luke — second memory reconstruction
 VISUAL Dry river in the present. Abandoned boat. Luke is nearby. Echo Lantern reacts. 
 PLAYER Activate Echo Lantern. 
 VISUAL Night. The river is flowing. Luke is helping villagers leave Veyra. 
 VISUAL People get into boats. Luke pushes one away. 
 VISUAL The river changes unnaturally. 
 VISUAL Luke sees a figure returning toward Veyra carrying something. The face cannot be seen. 
 VISUAL Luke checks his pocket watch: 2:31 AM. 
 VISUAL The village clock tower behind him reads 2:17 AM. 
 VISUAL The figure continues toward the village. The memory collapses. 
 SFX CRACK 
 Narration Luke's watch said 2:31. 
 Narration The village clock said 2:17. 
 Narration The night continued after the clocks stopped. 
 Casebook: IVY → 2:17 bell   CONTRADICTS   LUKE → 2:31 watch
 PLAYER Return to village investigation. 
 7. Hanna — identity and memory reconstruction
 DISCOVERY The abandoned room contains village archives. An old document identifies the unknown woman as HANNA — VILLAGE ARCHIVIST. 
 Narration The woman from earlier was Hanna. 
 VISUAL The Echo Lantern reacts in the archive. 
 PLAYER Activate Echo Lantern. 
 VISUAL Hanna is inside the village archive at night, examining old records. 
 HANNA The lantern was never meant to be used like this. 
 VISUAL She hears something underground. 
 VISUAL Hanna discovers a hidden entrance and enters. 
 VISUAL The same strange symbols cover the walls. 
 VISUAL Hanna reaches a chamber. An Echo Lantern is active. Someone is standing beside it; the face is hidden. 
 HANNA Stop. 
 SFX Flash / memory collapse. 
 Casebook: HANNA → archive → Echo Lantern → unknown person
