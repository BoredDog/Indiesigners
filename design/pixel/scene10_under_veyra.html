Under Veyra

The blood-stained key fits a hidden lock at the old well. CLICK. "The key wasn't meant for a door above ground." Dark tunnels, water, collapsed passages, strange symbols, abandoned rooms, and hundreds of Echo Lantern records.

One 16 × 16 stone tileset builds the tunnels, the lantern archive and the deepest chamber, so all three read as the same place. Elias's lantern is the only light.

Tunnel → lantern archive · Wide

30 × 17 tiles (480 × 272, crop 2 px). Elias's lantern sweeps left to right; symbols show only inside it, water drips and ripples, one niche lantern answers with its own glow.

Tileset · 16 × 16

Shown at 4×. Row 1 walls · row 2 floors and water · row 3 lantern archive · row 4 well and props. Tiles marked "residue" carry symbols that only appear inside the lantern light (separate residue sheet, same grid).

The well lock · CLICK
