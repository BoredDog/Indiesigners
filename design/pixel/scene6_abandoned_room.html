The Abandoned Room

Elias enters an abandoned room. A woman is sitting there. "Whatever you find here, don't trust the first memory you see." She disappears without fading. The chair remains and slowly rocks. Creeeak…

The room is the village archive under dust sheets, which pays off in scene 9. The woman sits in moonlight. Elias's lantern light comes in from the doorway on the left.
