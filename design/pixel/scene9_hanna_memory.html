Hanna's Archive

Hanna in the archive at night, examining records. "The lantern was never meant to be used like this." A sound underground. A hidden entrance behind a shelf. Symbols on every wall. A chamber: an Echo Lantern is active, and someone stands beside it, face hidden. "Stop." Flash.

Two pages. The archive is warm (her candle). The chamber below is all teal, the first place the lantern's colour fills a whole frame.

Page 1 · The archive

Loop: 0–3 s she reads; 3 s dust falls from the floor (the sound below); 4.5 s the right-hand shelf swings open and teal light spills up the stairs.

Page 2 · The chamber

Loop: the symbols pulse with the lantern. At 5 s, "Stop.": a 3-frame white-teal flash, then the memory collapses. The figure has the same hat-and-coat outline as Elias, but only from behind.
