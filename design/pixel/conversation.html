Talking to the Dead

The conversation screen for each witness, and the three text boxes used everywhere: narration caption, speech bubble, thought bubble.

The witness speaks from a framed portrait on the left, the questions sit in a list on the right, and the line being spoken fills a box along the bottom. The background is their place in the present day: dark, cold, no lamp.

Conversation · Ivy · Luke · Hanna

Portrait: the 64 × 64 bust at ×2 inside ui_portrait_frame_9s, washed to the ghost ramp in the present day. The name tab carries the witness accent (Ivy blue, Luke olive, Hanna violet). The hovered question gets the bone row and a ▸ marker.

Text boxes

Caption: bone paper, ink rule, for narration ("The clocks said 2:17."). Speech: rounded bone bubble plus a tail sprite. Ghost speech: the same shape in mist with a dusk outline, for the dead. Thought: dotted inner rule plus a three-dot trail.
