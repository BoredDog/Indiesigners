The Cast

The three witnesses as 64 × 64 conversation busts, plus the sister and the unknown woman as full-body sprites on the same 32 × 58 frame as Elias.

Drawn in code from flat shapes, auto ink outline and a hard shadow on the side away from the light. Paint over them: keep the canvas size, the palette and the head position so they drop straight into the game.

Portraits · 64 × 64

Shown at 5×. Order Bhumi paints them: Hanna first (Vansh needs her for the unknown woman), then Ivy, then Luke. In the present day the game washes them pale in code; the art stays in full colour for the memories.

Sprites · 32 × 58

Shown at 5×, feet on y = 56 like Elias. The unknown woman is Hanna in a pale, colourless variant: same outline, so the reveal in scene 9 lands.
