The Clock Tower

The clock reads 2:17 AM. The second hand is frozen. DONG. DONG. "The clock tower had been silent for ten years. Tonight, it rang again."

Same palette and rules as Scene 1. No lantern here, so nothing teal: the only warm colour is the bell, and it comes from sound, not light.

Shot 2A · Low angle

480 × 270 at 4×. The camera looks up from the street, so the tower narrows toward the top. Each DONG: the bell swings, amber rings spread, dust drops off the ledges, the screen shakes for a third of a second. Crows leave on the first one.

Shot 2B · The face

Push in on the face. The dried-blood second hand tries to move to the next tick, and snaps back every time. This is the only moving part of a clock that has been stopped for ten years.

192 × 108 at 10×. Hold under the narration line "Tonight, it rang again."
