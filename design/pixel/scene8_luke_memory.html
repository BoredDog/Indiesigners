Luke's River

Night. The river is flowing. Luke helps villagers leave Veyra and pushes a boat off. The river changes unnaturally. A figure walks back toward the village carrying something. Luke's watch says 2:31; the tower behind him says 2:17. CRACK.

The water carries the scene. It flows right, stops dead, then runs backwards with teal in it. That is the only teal until the figure passes on the far bank.

Close-up · The contradiction

Luke's gloved hand holds his pocket watch at 2:31. Over his shoulder, small and sharp, the tower says 2:17. Both clocks must be readable in one frame, because the casebook entry CONTRADICTION comes from this shot.

192 × 108 at 10×. The watch's second hand still moves. The tower's does not.
