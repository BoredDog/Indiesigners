Ivy's Schoolhouse

Night. Ivy is not teaching. She is alone, packing school materials to leave. Movement outside: everyone runs toward the square. DONG. "Someone rang the bell. I don't know who." A lantern in the crowd. The clock: 2:17. The memory glitches and freezes.

The memory is lit by Ivy's oil lamp, so it is warm inside. Teal appears once: the lantern carried through the crowd, seen through the window for a moment.
