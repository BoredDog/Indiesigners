Echo Paths

The puzzle board in the same pixels as the scenes: a stone floor, tall pillars and crates that throw ink shadows, gates, water and switches, the wisp and the sentinels.

16 × 16 tiles at ×4 make 64 px cells. Group colours (which lever opens which gate) are a 2 × 2 px marker the code draws in the tile's top-left corner, so one gate tile serves every group.

Sample board · pz_sis_2

Light from the west, shadow length 3: ink falls east of each pillar. The wisp bobs, the goal pulses, the lever and gates use the group markers (blood g1, blue g2).

Tiles · 8 × 4 sheet
